# Activity: What Must Happen Before a New Employee Can Start Work?

## Why you are doing this

This activity helps you see that a simple first day at work depends on people, processes, applications, data, devices, networks, security, and support. Draw what you understand; neatness is less important than showing the dependencies.

**Time:** 15–20 minutes
**Work alone, or in a small group of 3–4.**

## The setup

Pick any large company you can picture clearly — a bank, an airline, a hospital, a college, a retail chain, an energy company, or the one from tonight's story. Imagine one new employee joining that company on Monday morning. Your job is to figure out everything that has to happen — before, and on, their first day — for them to actually be able to do their job.

You already saw one version of this list in tonight's session. Don't just copy it. Think it through yourself, for the company you picked, and see how much of it you can rebuild — plus anything tonight's story didn't mention.

## Expected output

By the end, you should have a short, filled-in worksheet — not a polished document — that shows:

- At least 5 people/roles
- At least 5 ordered steps
- At least 2 applications
- At least 3 pieces of information
- At least 2 pieces of hardware
- At least 2 security requirements
- 2 realistic failure scenarios
- At least 1 support team

## Before you finish, ask yourself

Look back at your own worksheet. Is this really "one laptop request" — or is it a small system, with people, process, data, hardware, and rules all depending on each other? Write one sentence at the bottom of your worksheet answering that question in your own words. Bring it to Session 2.
