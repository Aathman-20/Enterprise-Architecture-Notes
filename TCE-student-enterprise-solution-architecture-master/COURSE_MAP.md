## The simple flow

Start with the business. Follow the work. Find the data. Place the technology. Protect and operate it. Record the tradeoffs. Then explain the whole thing to someone who was not in the room.

## If you feel lost

Return to these five questions:

1. What business result are we trying to improve?
2. Who is affected?
3. What happens from start to finish?
4. What information and systems does each step need?
5. What happens when volume grows or something fails?

If your team can answer those five questions clearly, the diagram will become much easier.
